Every setting

The name, standard value and purpose of every setting you can change.

The earlier chapters describe the settings of each subject where you meet it. This chapter lists them all in one table, in the order of the alphabet, so that you can look one up by name. The table is made from the program each time this manual is made, so it never lists a setting that Tuatara does not have.

A setting is a name that starts and ends with a star, such as *auto-save*. You change one in your init file, as in (setf *auto-save* nil). The table has three columns. The first is the name of the setting. The second is its standard value, the one Tuatara has until you change it, and a long value is cut short with three dots. The third says what the setting changes.

Only the settings that Tuatara promises to keep are listed. A setting that is not here may change from one version to the next, and should not be set in an init file you keep for years.

To find a setting from within the editor, press C-h v and type a few letters of its name. Tuatara shows its value and its full description. To find the settings that mention a word, press C-h a and type the word.

*archive-free-reserve*1073741824The octets left free on the drive an archive is unpacked onto (1 GB): unpacking stops before the drive has less, so that an archive holding far more than it takes up cannot fill the drive.
*archive-listing-limit*67108864The most octets of an archive's list of contents shown (64 MB); a longer one is not shown, and that is said.
*archive-seconds*86400The seconds tar, 7-Zip or bsdtar may take to make, list or unpack one archive before it is ended: a day, for the largest; M-x stop-job ends it sooner.
*auto-save*tTrue for buffers visiting files to have their changes not saved written now and then to a copy of their own in AUTO-SAVE-DIRECTORY, never to the file itself; M-x recover-file takes such a copy back.
*auto-save-directory*nilThe folder auto-saved copies are kept in, made for you alone when it is not there: NIL for auto-save/ in Tuatara's state folder ($XDG_STATE_HOME/tuatara/, or ~/.local/state/tuatara/), or a folder's name, "~/backups/tuatara/".
*auto-save-idle*5Seconds without a change after which a buffer's changes not saved are auto-saved (AUTO-SAVE), 5 as standard.
*auto-save-interval*60The most seconds changes not saved go without an auto-save while they go on without a pause (AUTO-SAVE), 60 as standard.
*auto-save-size-limit*268435456The largest buffer auto-saved, in octets, 256 MB as standard: a larger one is not copied (each copy would be written whole), so a crash loses its changes not saved.
*auto-scratch-buffers*("*lisp-scratch*" "*org-scratch*")The scratch buffers made without being asked for: at startup, and when the last buffer is killed.
*blink-matching-paren*tTrue to say, when a closing bracket is typed, what its partner is when it is out of sight ("Matches (defun foo ()"), or that it has none.
*bookmark-set-asks-categories*tTrue for M-x bookmark-set to ask, after the name, which categories the bookmark is in (those used already offered, new ones typed); NIL never to ask.
